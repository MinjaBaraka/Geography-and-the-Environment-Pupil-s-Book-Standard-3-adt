Geography and the Environment Pupil’s Book Standard 3

Environmental conservation is the act of protecting and preserving the things that surround human beings. The things includes forests, land, rivers, valleys, animals, mountains, air and oceans. Environmental conservation activities in Tanzania are carried out at different levels. The government has established institutions and offices to conserve the environment. There are environmental conservation offices at village, street and ward levels. There are also environmental conservation offices at district, regional and national levels. The offices at different levels are managed by environmental officers. The officers collaborate with government leaders to manage the environment.

Moreover, at each level there are environmental management committees. The committees include that of health and the environment. These committees are responsible to mobilize communities to participate in environmental management activities. These activities include planting and caring for trees. It is the responsibility of the government at each level to make by-laws and impose fines on anyone caught destroying the environment. In addition, the government of Tanzania established the National Environmental Management Council (NEMC) in 1983 so that it could advise the government on matters pertaining to environmental management. Thus, we are supposed to conserve our environment to protect ourselves and other organisms.
