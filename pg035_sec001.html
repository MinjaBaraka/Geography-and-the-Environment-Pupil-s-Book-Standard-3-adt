Chapter Three

Environmental conservation

Introduction

The well-being of humans and other organisms depends on essential resources such as land, air and water which are found in their environment. Sustainable use of these resources depends on environmental conservation. Therefore, it is important for the society to understand environmental conservation so as to ensure sustainable utilization of available resources. In this chapter, you will learn about the meaning and importance of conserving the environment, and learn various ways of conserving the environment. The competencies to be developed will enable you to conserve the environment so as to achieve sustainable development.
