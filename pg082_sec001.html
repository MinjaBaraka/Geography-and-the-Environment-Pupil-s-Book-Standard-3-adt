The air pollution caused by greenhouse gases from industries, the aviation industry and vehicles which contribute to global warming should be controlled.

In farming, people have to use appropriate industrial fertilisers, especially those rich in nitrogen. This is because the nitrous oxide gas emitted during agricultural activities contributes to global warming when it is released into the atmosphere. Therefore, farmers are advised to practise organic farming, which is sustainable and environment-friendly.

Adaptation to the effects of climate change

Climate change often affects the environment surrounding us. The effects of climate change such as drought, extreme heat and floods pose serious challenges to humans such as famine and diseases. Since climate change will continue to exist, it is necessary to adapt to both its current and future effects. Some of the strategies for adapting to the effects of climate change are planting drought-tolerant and disease-resistant crops; and crops that mature early such as millet, sweet potatoes, cassava, pulses and sorghum. In areas where irrigation is practised, it is important to use the drip irrigation system that uses a little amount of water. Furthermore, the harvesting and storage of rain water for use during water shortages is another way of adapting to the effects of climate change.
