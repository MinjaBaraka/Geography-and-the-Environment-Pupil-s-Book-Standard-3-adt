Geography and the Environment Pupil’s Book Standard 3

It is important to keep the classroom clean all the time. Before cleaning the classroom floor, you should clean the windows, the corners the ceiling, the chalkboard, the doors and walls. After cleaning those areas, clean the floor and finally clean the environment outside the classroom.

The tools that may be used for cleaning include a broom, a bucket for keeping clean water, a dusting cloth, a cobweb duster and a mopper. Other tools include a powder or liquid detergent, a duster, a dust pan and a dust bin. Dust bins should be differentiated using colours and labels to show the type of waste that should be disposed of in the bins.

Furthermore, we should know the steps to follow during cleaning. The important steps are:

(a)
Removing cobwebs from the ceiling, windows, doors, corners and walls
(b)
Cleaning the chalkboard
(c)
Dusting-off desks and windows
(d)
Sweeping the floor
(e)
Collecting and sorting waste into waste that decompose and that which does not, and disposing them in appropriate bins
(f)
Mopping the classroom floor
(g)
Arranging the desks and tables
(h)
Sweeping the classroom and picking-up garbages
