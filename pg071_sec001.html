Ways of controlling the effects of industrial activities on the environment

The Environmental effects of manufacturing activities can be controlled by locating industries far away from human settlements. Industries should establish a system of treating and cleaning waste water for reuse. Industries should reduce emissions by having in place smoke-cleaning technologies for cleaning up toxic substances and dust before they are released into the atmosphere. Industries should plan for recycling waste that can be used for other purposes. For example, used paper can be recycled to make new paper. Paper can also be used to make charcoal energy. Other kinds of garbage can be transformed into organic fertilisers. In addition, industries should install soundproof systems to reduce noise pollution.

Mining

Mining is a process of extracting useful substances from the earth. Examples of substances that are mined are coal, gold, diamond, gravel, salt and sand. These substances are called minerals. Minerals are naturally-occurring resources found on land. Tanzania is rich in minerals such as gold, diamond, iron, coal, nickel, tanzanite and uranium.
