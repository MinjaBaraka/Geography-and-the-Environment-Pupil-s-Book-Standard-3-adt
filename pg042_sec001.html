The importance of cleaning the classroom

Cleaning the classroom is important for the well-being of pupils in a learning environment. We clean classrooms in order to maintain the classroom infrastructure so that it lasts longer. We also clean the classroom to remove dust that can cause respiratory diseases such as asthma, cough and flu. Furthermore, we clean the classroom so as to learn in a clean and safe environment. Moreover, cleaning the classroom helps a pupil to develop a habit of cleaning in his or her environment.
