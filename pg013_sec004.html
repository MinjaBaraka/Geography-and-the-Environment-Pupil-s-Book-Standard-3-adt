Relationship between Geography and the Environment

Geography and the Environment are related concepts that are difficult to separate.We have learnt that the environment encompasses all the things that surround us, while Geography explains those things.Therefore, Geography helps us to understand the environment that surrounds human beings and how to interact with them. Things like the forests, mountains, lakes, rivers, oceans, weather, land and animals around human beings constitute what we call is Geography and the Environment as a discipline. Therefore, it is important to study both at the same time.
