Environmental conservation by planting trees, grass and flowers

Trees, grass and flowers are plants which are attractive and which beautify the environment. Trees also help to regulate the weather and provide shades, and some flowers smell nice. A place with flowers that smell nice attracts living organisms such as butterflies, bees and birds. When trees, grasses and flowers disappear, the environment may experience drought.

Planting trees, grass and flowers

Before planting trees, grass and flowers, you should follow several steps. First, you need to know the types of grass, flowers and trees that are suitable in the environment. There are plants that require more water to thrive, while others need less water. Choosing appropriate trees, grass and flowers depends on the area where they will be planted. It is better to include some fruit trees.
