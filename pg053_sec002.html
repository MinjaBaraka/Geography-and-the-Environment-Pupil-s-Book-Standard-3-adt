Vocabulary

Cobweb

a net of sticky threads made by a spider to catch insects

Compost

a manure made from organic wastes such as grasses, farm residues and fallen leaves that rot in a special bin or pit

Garden

the area of land next to a house, where flowers, grass and other plants are grown

Mop

a rag used for washing floors which consists of a long stick with threads of thick strings or sponge fastened to one end
