Fishing

Fishing is an economic activity that involves harvesting aquatic organisms, including fish. Fishing takes place in marine and fresh-water environments. Salt water sources include oceans, while fresh water sources are rivers, lakes, ponds and dams. Some of the salt-water fish are snapper, rabbit fish, octopuses, mackerel, jacks, kingfish and sharks. Examples of freshwater fish are tilapia, catfish and the Nile perch. Tanzania is rich in fishing areas, including the Indian Ocean, Lake Victoria, Lake Tanganyika and Lake Nyasa. Others are the River Kagera, River Mara, River Rufiji, Great Ruaha River, River Wami and River Pangani.
