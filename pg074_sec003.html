Fishing

Fishing is an economic activity that involves harvesting aquatic organisms, including fish. Fishing takes place in marine and fresh-water environments. Salt water sources include oceans, while fresh water sources are rivers, lakes, ponds and dams. Some of the salt-water fish are snapper, rabbit fish, octopuses, mackerel, jacks, kingfish and sharks. Examples of freshwater fish are tilapia, catfish and the Nile perch. Tanzania is rich in fishing areas, including the Indian Ocean, Lake Victoria, Lake Tanganyika and Lake Nyasa. Others are the River Kagera, River Mara, River Rufiji, Great Ruaha River, River Wami and River Pangani. Mtera, Nyumba ya Mungu and Kidatu are some of the dams used for fishing in Tanzania.

Advantages of fishing

Fishing activities have many advantages. The provision of food and employment is one of advantages of fishing. Communities that live near fishing areas can engage in fishing and fish vending. Fishing provides raw materials for industries that process fish. Fishing contributes to government revenue through taxation. In addition, fishing provides foreign exchange through the export of fish and fish products.

Effects of fishing on the environment

Fishermen use different ways to catch fish. Fishing methods can be appropriate or inappropriate to the environment. Appropriate fishing methods include the use of proper nets that are standard and ideal for fishing. Inappropriate fishing methods are those that damage fish and destroy their breeding grounds. These methods involve using poisons and explosives. Poisons and explosives destroy marine and fresh-water environments. Poisons and explosives kill fish and other aquatic organisms. The breeding grounds for aquatic organisms are also destroyed by poisons and expolosives, leading to the extinction of marine life. Poisoned fish, in turn, affect consumers by causing diseases such as cancer. Moreover, fishing using seine nets is one of the improper fishing ways because it catches everything, including small fish, eggs and other aquatic organisms.
