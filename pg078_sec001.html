Advantages of lumbering

Lumbering provides employment to the people who are involved in lumbering and related activities. It is a source of government revenue obtained by taxing the people, companies and industries involved in lumbering. It provides foreign exchange through the export of timber. It provides raw materials like timber for making paper and for timber processing industries. Timber is also used in the construction of canoes, boats and houses. Timber can also be used to make furniture such as tables, chairs, cabinets, boards, doors and cupboards. Furthermore, trees help to control soil erosion and serve as wind breakers.

Effects of lumbering

Lumbering involves cutting down trees something that can cause environmental degradation. The areas where trees are cut remain bare, leading to soil erosion. Illegal lumbering causes deforestation and loss of the living organisms that depend on forests. Lumbering destroys habitats for animals and birds. This may lead to extinction of the living organisms that depend on forests. Illegal lumbering can also limit the supply of timber and wood, and ultimately the loss of government revenue.

Ways of addressing the effects of lumbering

There are different ways of dealing with the effects of lumbering on the environment. The ways include planting trees in deforested areas. People should be advised to use non-wood materials for construction purposes. The laws and regulations on forest harvesting should be enforced. Education on the effects of deforestation should be provided to communities.
