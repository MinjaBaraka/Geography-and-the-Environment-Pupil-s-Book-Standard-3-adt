Land conservation in our environment

Activity 12

Observe the condition of the land in the area you live in.

Then:

1. With examples, give a summary of the condition of the land in your environment; and

2. Suggest ways to conserve the land in the area you live in.

Land is essential for the humans and other organisms. Plants grow and thrive on land and, in turn, we obtain food from plants. The land contains minerals such as gold, diamond tin. When they are mined, minerals are sold to generate income. Land has water that we use in doing various activities. Human beings and other living organisms find their habitats on land.

Since land is important to our lives, it is a good idea to ensure that land is not degraded. There are various ways to conserve land. The ways may vary depending on the environment and the specific needs of the area. Land can be conserved in the following ways:

The conservation of forests by preventing the random cutting of trees and the promotion of tree planting should be emphasised. These prevent soil erosion and enable soil to retain moisture. Controlling the environmental pollution caused by industries, agriculture and other human activities is also necessary for maintaining soil fertility and the quality land. Planting trees in bare land improves the quality of soil and controls soil erosion.

Land conservation can also be achieved by adopting sustainable agricultural practices such as the use of organic manure. Likewise, we conserve land by avoiding inappropriate construction of infrastructure and mining practices that destroy the land. Generally, land is conserved by ensuring that waste is collected and properly managed to prevent pollution.

Rights and obligations in environmental conservation

The Environmental Management Act of 2004, provides rights to everyone living in Tanzania so that everyone lives in a clean, safe and healthy environment. The Act also provides responsibilities to everyone living in Tanzania to safeguard and maintain the environment and to inform the relevant authority about any activities or practices that may affect the environment.
