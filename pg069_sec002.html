Industries

An industry is a place where machines are used to process and produce products.

The products may be clothes, mattresses, vehicles, motorcycles, foods and drinks.

There are two types of industries.

The first type includes industries which process raw materials.

The second includes industries which produce consumable goods.

An example of a raw material-processing industry is a cotton processing industry that produces cotton fibers.

Examples of industries that produce goods from raw materials or products from processing industries are textile industries that produce clothes from cotton fibers.

A carpet-producing industry uses sisal fibers to produce carpets.

Moreover, a sugar industry uses sugarcane to produce sugar.

Advantages of industries

Industries help to process and transform raw materials into finished and semi-finished products for different uses.

Industries help to increase the national income through taxation.

The country also earns foreign exchange through the export of industrial products.
