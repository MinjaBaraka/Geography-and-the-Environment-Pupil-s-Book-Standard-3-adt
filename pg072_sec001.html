Advantages of mining

Mining provides employment to people and raw materials for different industries. Minerals such as gold and diamond are used as ornaments. Salt is used for flavouring food, as medicine and for preserving food such as fish and meat. Diamond is used as an industrial abrasive for cutting hard materials like glass and for drilling rocks.
