Advantages of farming

Agriculture provides us with food and raw materials, which can be processed in industries. The selling of food and cash crops also provides us with local and foreign currency. Generally, agriculture provides employment and it is a national identity.

Effects of poor farming practices on the environment

Farming if not practised properly, can destroy the environment. For example, farming on mountain slopes causes soil erosion. Shifting cultivation that involves cutting down trees and burning grass and forests causes the loss of soil fertility, drought and soil erosion. Improper use of industrial fertilisers, pesticides and herbicides causes the loss of soil fertility and destroys soil living organisms.
