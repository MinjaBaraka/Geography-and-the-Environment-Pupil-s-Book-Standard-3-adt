Exercise 1

If you were given an opportunity to educate members of your community about appropriate use of water, what would you ask them to consider?

In order to reduce water loss, we must use water carefully, like closing water taps after use.

Similarly, we are supposed to repair damaged pipes and maintain proper use of water from different sources.

We are also supposed to report to the water authorities any incidents of broken water pipes. Furthermore, the farmers doing irrigation farming are supposed to use sustainable irrigation technologies that save water.
