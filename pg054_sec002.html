Chapter Four

Environmental degradation

Introduction

Human beings and other organisms need a clean environment for their well-being and growth. Environmental degradation affects the well-being of human beings and other organisms. Therefore, it is important to conserve the environment for the well-being of human beings and other organisms. In this chapter, you will learn about the human activities that destroy the environment and the effects of environmental degradation on human beings and other organisms. You will also learn how environmental degradation affects climate. The competencies to be developed will enable you to avoid environmental degradation in the environment you live in.
