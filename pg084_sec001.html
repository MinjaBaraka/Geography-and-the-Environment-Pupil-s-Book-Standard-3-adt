Vocabulary

Abrasive

a substance used for grinding, polishing or cleaning a hard surface

Backfilling

a process of returning materials removed during extraction into the underground part of a mine

Breeding area

a special environment suitable for fish to lay eggs and for the growth of young fish

Energy

any material that can be used to generate electricity and other forms of power

Greenhouse gases

gasses in the earth’s atmosphere which trap heat

Kraal

an area surrounded by a barrier used for keeping livestock

Mercury

a liquid metal used by small-scale miners in extraction of gold

Seine net

a fishing net that is used to encircle fish or other aquatic organisms
