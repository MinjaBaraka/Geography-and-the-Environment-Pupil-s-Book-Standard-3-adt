There are various sources of environmental degradation. These include littering; smoke from factories, vehicles and homes; dust from mines and industries; and waste water from industries, mining areas and homes. Other kind of environmental degradation result from defecating, bathing, washing clothes, cultivating and watering livestock in water sources, overuse and misuse of water, excessive cutting down of trees and the burning of forests.

Waste disposal

When human beings undertake various activities, they produce wastes. The waste can be in solid or liquid form. Some solid waste can decompose, while other kinds may not. Examples of wastes that decompose easily are food and plant remains. Examples of wastes that do not decompose easily are plastic bags, plastic buckets, plastic bottles and plastic lids.

Plastic waste destroys the environment because it takes a long time to decompose. Types of waste that do not decompose quickly affect soil fertility, prevent water penetration into the soil, prevent plants from growing and affect the life of animals in the areas they live in.
