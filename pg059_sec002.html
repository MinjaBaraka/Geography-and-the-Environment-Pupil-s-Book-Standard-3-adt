To avoid pollution of water sources, we should use improved toilets and latrines, dispose of wastes in designated places, bathing in a shower and washing clothes away from water sources. Also, we should avoid watering livestock in water sources. In addition, laws that prohibit bad human practises near or in water sources should be enforced.

Overuse and misuse of water

Water is essential for humans and other living organisms. Despite its importance, there is a high loss of water in our environment. The loss is due to overuse and misuse of water, for example leaving and neglecting leaking pipes and not closing water taps. Likewise, irrigation agriculture can use a large amount of water from rivers and dams.
